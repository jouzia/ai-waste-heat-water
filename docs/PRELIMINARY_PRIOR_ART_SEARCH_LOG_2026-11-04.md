## Kanbur access audit

The SDU institutional record identifies Fei Duan as corresponding author and provides a library access-check link. The page does not expose a downloadable full-text file or supplement. The document-delivery listing identifies the exact article and a paid delivery option; it does not contain the article itself.

No purchase was made and no author was contacted. No claim is made that the article or supplement is unavailable through all institutional subscriptions or author-held copies. A next access attempt should use an eligible institutional library subscription/interlibrary loan or an explicitly authorized author request.

## Screening decisions and limits

- Kanbur (2020) remains a **direct prior-art threat** for the broad concept of data-center waste-heat-driven DCMD and thermoeconomic optimization.
- Lei et al. (2025) is relevant to workload-level water accounting, but the abstract alone does not establish its exact operational water boundary or time resolution.
- Katakam et al. (2026/2027) is a direct system-scale desalination-for-data-centers prior-art threat, but the abstract alone is insufficient for equation- and boundary-level comparison.
- Mytton (2021) is retained as foundational context for direct versus electricity-related water accounting; it does not establish novelty for this integrated MD system.
- Evaristo (2026) is marked preprint/unreviewed and must be kept separate from peer-reviewed evidence.
- No result in this log establishes novelty, model validation, net freshwater benefit, or complete literature coverage.
