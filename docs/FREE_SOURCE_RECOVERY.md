# Free-source recovery plan for Martínez-Díez & Vázquez-González (1999)

Date: 2026-09-30

## Objective

Recover enough legally accessible evidence to support publication-grade validation without requiring a paid copy of the 1999 article.

## Evidence currently available

- The ScienceDirect article record exposes substantial experimental text and confirms the TF200 PTFE membrane, 80% void fraction, 60 micrometre thickness, 0.2 micrometre nominal pore size, nine feed and nine permeate channels, 55.0 x 7.0 x 0.45 mm channel dimensions, and effective membrane area of 33.7 x 10^-4 m^2.
- The same source states that measured fluxes are averages of at least three experiments, with bulk inlet/outlet temperatures maintained within +/-0.1 C and recirculation rates and concentrations stable within 1%.
- An open-access 2021 technical review independently reports the NaCl concentrations used in the 1999 experiments: 0, 0.55, 1.15 and 1.67 mol/L. It also reports the qualitative decline in permeate flux with increasing concentration.

## What we will NOT do

Do not copy numerical observations from secondary prose, model curves, or inferred values into the primary observation ledger. Do not treat a later paper's reproduction as primary-source validation.

## What remains needed

1. A legally accessible copy or image of the original 1999 figures/tables.
2. Numerical observed flux points with source locators.
3. Matching feed/permeate temperatures and recirculation conditions.
4. Figure digitization records when values are extracted from plots.
5. Independent repeat extraction for a predefined subset.
6. Uncertainty from digitization and unit transformation.

## Fallback validation route

If the original quantitative observations remain inaccessible, keep the 1999 benchmark provenance-established but quantitatively blocked. Proceed with independent open-access experimental datasets for the unfitted model comparison and use the 1999 paper only for source-grounded setup/equation context.

## Cost policy

No paper purchase is required for the project to continue. Any source that requires payment is treated as optional unless the missing information becomes essential and no lawful alternative exists.
