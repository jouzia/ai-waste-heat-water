## Ali, Orfi & Najib (2020)

Primary article: Ali, E., Orfi, J., & Najib, A. (2020), "Developing and validating a dynamic model of water production by direct-contact membrane distillation", PLOS ONE 15(3), e0230207, DOI 10.1371/journal.pone.0230207.

The publisher explicitly provides five supporting XLSX data objects:
- S1 Data: DOI 10.1371/journal.pone.0230207.s002
- S2 Data: DOI 10.1371/journal.pone.0230207.s003
- S3 Data: DOI 10.1371/journal.pone.0230207.s004
- S4 Data: DOI 10.1371/journal.pone.0230207.s005
- S5 Data: DOI 10.1371/journal.pone.0230207.s006

The associated NLM dataset catalogue identifies the Figshare record as dataset 12025557 and records CC BY 4.0.

The paper reports a 10 m² pilot DCMD module, 230 μm membrane thickness, 14 m channel length, 0.7 m channel height, 0.2 μm pore diameter, 0.8 porosity, and 4.1 bar water-entry pressure. Experiments used equal hot/cold flow rates, a nominal 25 °C cold inlet, 0.5% feed salinity, and 10 s sampling. The paper states that adding a heat-loss term reduced the reported model–plant mismatch to approximately 3%.
