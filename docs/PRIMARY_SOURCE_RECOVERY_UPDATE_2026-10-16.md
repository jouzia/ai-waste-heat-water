# Primary-source recovery update — Martínez-Díez & Vázquez-González (1999)

**Checked:** 2026-10-16

## What changed

A targeted search found the publisher's ScienceDirect article page for:

L. Martínez-Díez and M. I. Vázquez-González (1999), “Temperature and concentration polarization in membrane distillation of aqueous salt solutions,” *Journal of Membrane Science* 156(2), 265–273. DOI: https://doi.org/10.1016/S0376-7388(98)00349-4. Publisher page: https://www.sciencedirect.com/science/article/abs/pii/S0376738898003494

The page exposes indexed article text beyond the abstract, including an **Experimental** section snippet that identifies a flat PTFE membrane, Gelman TF200, 80% void fraction, 60 μm thickness, and **0.2 μm nominal pore size**. This supports resolving the secondary-source “0.2 mm” statement as a likely transcription/typographical error, but the conflict must remain documented until the primary PDF itself is inspected. Do not use “0.2 mm” as a model parameter.

The publisher page also confirms the paper discusses water and aqueous NaCl feed, measured flux results, temperature and concentration polarization, and variation across temperatures, recirculation rates, and solution concentrations.
