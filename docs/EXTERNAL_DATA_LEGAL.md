# External Data Legal and Attribution Protocol

This research uses publicly documented scientific and government datasets under their applicable licenses and terms. Public accessibility is not treated as unrestricted redistribution.

## WRI Aqueduct 4.0

WRI states that Aqueduct 4.0 data may be shared, reproduced, and adapted with attribution under the Creative Commons Attribution International 4.0 License. WRI asks users adapting or sharing the data to register with WRI and provide attribution.

Required project attribution:

> Source: WRI Aqueduct, accessed on [insert retrieval date]. Licensed under Creative Commons Attribution International 4.0. Used for basin-level water-risk screening and scenario stratification; Aqueduct indicators are not treated as site-specific measurements.

Official sources:
- https://datasets.wri.org/datasets/aqueduct-global-maps-40-data
- https://www.wri.org/aqueduct/faq
- https://www.wri.org/research/aqueduct-40-updated-decision-relevant-global-water-risk-indicators

## NASA POWER / MERRA-2

NASA POWER documentation identifies its meteorological parameters as derived from GMAO/MERRA-2 assimilation data and documents spatial, temporal, and validation characteristics. The project records the exact POWER product, parameter, date range, retrieval date, and source information.

Official documentation:
- https://power.larc.nasa.gov/docs/methodology/
- https://power.larc.nasa.gov/docs/methodology/meteorology/
- https://power.larc.nasa.gov/docs/services/api/

## Government of India open data

For India-specific government datasets, use the official data.gov.in or responsible department portal and record the dataset's applicable Government Open Data License - India terms. The data.gov.in platform states that published content is owned by the relevant ministry/state/department/organization and licensed under the Government Open Data License - India.

Official portal: https://data.gov.in/

## India-WRIS / CWC / NRSC

Use official India-WRIS/CWC/NRSC publications or data services. Record the exact report or dataset, release/version, access date, responsible agency, and reuse conditions. Do not infer that every derivative or third-party mirror has identical redistribution terms.

## General procedure

1. Obtain data from the official provider.
2. Record the license and attribution requirements shown by that provider.
3. Preserve version and retrieval date.
4. Record checksum where raw-file hashing is possible.
5. Keep raw data outside the repository when redistribution is not clearly permitted.
6. Publish only legally shareable derived data.
7. Preserve enough metadata and code for independent reconstruction.

If a portal requires registration or acceptance of terms in the user's name, the user must complete that action personally. Research tooling must never impersonate the user.
