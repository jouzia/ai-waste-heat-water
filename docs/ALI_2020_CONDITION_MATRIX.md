# Ali et al. (2020) operating-condition matrix

This matrix records the operating conditions visible in the article's described experimental range and response figures. It is a **case registry**, not a measurement ledger: no measured response values are present in this CSV.

Source: Ali, E., Orfi, J., & Najib, A. (2020). *Developing and validating a dynamic model of water production by direct-contact membrane distillation*. PLOS ONE 15(3), e0230207. DOI: https://doi.org/10.1371/journal.pone.0230207. CC BY 4.0.

The article states that hot- and cold-side flow rates were maintained equal; the cold inlet was nominally 25 °C; feed salinity was 0.5%; and sampling interval was 10 s. Figure 7 labels combinations of 50, 100, 200 and 300 L/h with hot inlet temperatures of 50, 60, 70 and 80 °C. Figures 2 and 3 show selected response plots for 50 and 300 L/h. Figure 7 provides model/plant response plots across the broader condition matrix.

## Use restrictions

- Do not treat a registered condition as an extracted observation.
- Do not infer precise raw time series from plot pixels without a separately documented digitization protocol and uncertainty.
- Do not fit or validate the project's MD model using this registry alone.
- Raw supporting XLSX files remain required for source-linked observations, exact timestamps, measured quantities, and uncertainty information.
- The case IDs are repository-generated identifiers, not source-assigned experiment IDs.

Machine-readable matrix: `01_literature/derived/ali_2020_condition_matrix.csv`.
