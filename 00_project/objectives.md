# Objectives

1. Construct an end-to-end model connecting AI workload energy to recoverable waste heat and freshwater production.
2. Parameterize the model from peer-reviewed literature, authoritative datasets, and documented public prototypes.
3. Quantify the effects of thermal quality, heat-recovery efficiency, cooling, pumping, salinity, and operating conditions.
4. Determine break-even regions where recovered freshwater exceeds additional water consumption.
5. Quantify uncertainty and identify dominant parameters using global sensitivity analysis.
6. Evaluate geographic variation using climate, water-stress, and electricity-water-intensity parameters.
7. Validate equations and selected model behavior against literature and, where feasible, controlled physical measurements.
8. Produce a reproducible research package and publication-ready manuscript.
