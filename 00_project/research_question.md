# Research Question

## Primary question

Under what combinations of AI workload intensity, data-center thermal conditions, waste-heat recovery efficiency, membrane-distillation operating conditions, cooling requirements, electricity-water intensity, and geographic water stress can waste-heat recovery produce a positive net freshwater benefit?

## Operational question

For a defined AI infrastructure scenario, does freshwater recovered through waste-heat-assisted thermal desalination exceed additional freshwater consumption attributable to the integrated recovery pathway?

## Boundary

The study will quantify, where data permit: IT electricity, associated heat generation, cooling energy and water, recoverable waste heat, heat-exchanger losses, membrane-distillation demand, pumping, auxiliary electricity, pretreatment, freshwater production, electricity-related indirect water consumption, and geographic water-stress context.

Withdrawal, consumption, and recovered freshwater will remain distinct quantities.
