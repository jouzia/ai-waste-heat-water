# Hypotheses

H1 — Higher recoverable heat temperature increases the feasible freshwater-production envelope, subject to membrane-distillation operating limits.

H2 — Higher AI utilization and workload intensity generally increase heat availability through increased IT energy use, but net freshwater benefit does not necessarily increase proportionally.

H3 — Cooling requirements can materially reduce or eliminate a gross freshwater benefit in some operating regimes.

H4 — Climate, local water stress, and electricity-water intensity materially change the net-water result for otherwise similar AI workloads.

H5 — Joint optimization of heat recovery, thermal management, membrane-distillation operation, and auxiliary loads can improve water-production efficiency.

H6 — There is no universal positive-benefit threshold. The system has a multidimensional feasibility envelope defined by thermal, hydraulic, electrical, climatic, and water-accounting conditions.
