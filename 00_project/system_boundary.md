# System Boundary

AI workload -> IT electricity -> heat generation -> cooling -> heat recovery -> heat exchanger -> membrane distillation -> freshwater output -> water accounting.

## Included burdens

- Cooling energy and water where applicable
- Heat-recovery and heat-exchanger losses
- Membrane-distillation heating and cooling requirements
- Pumping
- Auxiliary electricity
- Pretreatment where data are available
- Electricity-related indirect water consumption
- Feedwater and brine handling where material to the pathway

## Water accounting

The core analysis focuses on blue-water consumption while separately tracking withdrawal and recovered freshwater wherever data permit.

Generic net metric: W_net = W_recovered - W_additional_consumption.

The final sign convention and boundary will be defined explicitly in the model and sensitivity-tested.

## Exclusions

Full cradle-to-grave life-cycle assessment is not assumed. Embodied infrastructure impacts will only be included if sufficiently defensible inventories are available.
