# Terminology

## Water withdrawal
Water removed from a source, whether or not it is returned.

## Water consumption
Water withdrawn but not returned to the source in the relevant accounting boundary.

## Recovered freshwater
Freshwater produced by the recovery/desalination pathway.

## Recoverable waste heat
The fraction of generated thermal energy that can actually be transferred to the recovery process under defined temperature, flow, exchanger, and availability constraints.

## Net freshwater benefit
Recovered freshwater minus additional freshwater consumption attributable to the recovery pathway under the chosen accounting boundary.

Gross freshwater production is not a synonym for net water benefit.
